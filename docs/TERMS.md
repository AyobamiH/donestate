Last updated: 27 August 2026

[OpsTruth](https://opstruth.io) is the separate, independent read-only verification product. DoneState is currently provided as open-source preview software under the Apache-2.0 licence. Do not use the preview for illegal activity, unauthorised access, destructive operations or workloads requiring guarantees that are not expressly documented.

DoneState executes only within explicitly granted authority. It may stop a run when authority, capability or safety evidence is missing, and may classify uncertain external effects as `AMBIGUOUS_EFFECT`. `AWAITING_VERIFICATION` is not a guarantee that an objective is correct or complete. DoneState cannot prove its own completion: only an independent pinned attestation based on read-only verification can move the implemented state machine to `VERIFIED`, and that status still reflects the evidence and verifier used.
